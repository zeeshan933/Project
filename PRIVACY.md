## Privacy Policy for Moodle Dark Mode
Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the "Moodle Dark Mode" Chrome extension ("the Extension") handles your data. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how our Extension operates.

1. Data Collection
The Extension does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any of your personal data, browsing history, or user activity.

2. Data Storage and Usage
To function properly, the Extension requires the use of your browser's local storage API (chrome.storage). This is used strictly for the following purposes:

Saving Preferences: To store the specific Moodle domain names (e.g., lms.example.edu) that you manually add to the Extension.

Toggle State: To remember whether you have the master "Enable Dark Mode" switch turned on or off.

All of this data is stored locally on your device. It never leaves your browser and is never sent to any external servers or third parties.

3. Permissions Explained
The Extension requests the following permissions for these specific reasons:

storage: Necessary to save your customized domain list and toggle preferences locally on your machine.

Host Permissions: Necessary to detect when you navigate to a domain you have saved and to inject the dark mode styling specifically into those pages. The Extension only runs on the specific URLs you provide.

4. Third-Party Access
Because no data is collected or transmitted, no data is shared with or sold to third parties. We do not use analytics or tracking software within the Extension.

5. Changes to this Policy
If we make changes to how the Extension operates regarding your data, we will update this Privacy Policy and, if necessary, request your consent for new permissions through the Chrome Web Store update process.

6. Contact
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the Extension, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or contact the developer directly.
